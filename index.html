Job Scanner

Personal, locally operated job-search automation.

About

Job Scanner helps its owner organize job-search activity and perform actions that the owner explicitly requests. It is not offered as a public SaaS product.

Google integration

The Gmail integration is used only after the Google account owner grants access. It supports user-initiated email actions, such as sending an application email with an attachment, and the minimum mailbox access needed to verify and manage those actions.

OAuth credentials and tokens are kept in the owner’s local Hermes profile. Job Scanner does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.
