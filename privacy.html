Privacy Policy

Job Scanner · Effective September 16, 2026

1. Scope

This policy describes how Job Scanner handles Google user data. Job Scanner is a personal, locally operated tool used by its owner for job-search activity.

2. Google data accessed

After explicit OAuth consent, Job Scanner may request the following Gmail permissions:

  • gmail.send — send email only when the user requests an email action.
  • gmail.readonly — read message metadata or content when needed to verify user-requested email activity.
  • gmail.modify — manage messages or labels only when required by a user-requested workflow.

3. Use of data

Google user data is used only to provide the Gmail functionality explicitly requested by the account owner. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

4. Storage and security

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally in the owner’s Hermes profile. They are not committed to this public repository. Job Scanner does not operate a remote database that stores Gmail message content.

5. Sharing and sale

Job Scanner does not sell Google user data. It does not transfer Google user data to third parties except when necessary to complete an action explicitly requested by the user, such as delivering an email to its intended recipient, or when required by law.

6. Retention and deletion

Local OAuth tokens remain on the owner’s device until the owner deletes them or revokes access. Temporary message data is retained only as needed to complete and verify the requested action.

7. Revoking access

The owner may revoke Job Scanner’s access at any time from Google Account permissions and may delete the local OAuth token file.

8. Google API Services User Data Policy

Job Scanner’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

9. Contact

Questions may be submitted through the public GitHub repository linked from the project homepage.
